I enhanced my qualitative research skills while developing a quantitative ranking strategy to measure and mitigate 
a watershed’s pollutant, human-induced, and climate-caused stressor interactions. 
I reviewed an Illinois EPA Clean Water Act 303(d) report to identify pollutants and determine variables necessary 
to develop an equation for ranking the stressors. 
I then reviewed a Total Maximum Daily Load 2023 report to discern existing management plans. 
The final ranking was adjusted for local-level mitigation feasibility.
